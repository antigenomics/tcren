# Bundled TCRen2 potential

This is a frozen empirical residue-contact potential, not a binding free energy.
Use the packaged potential registry and SOURCES for the exact reference population,
cutoff, balancing, pseudocount and file identity. Do not infer the derivation from
the current contents of a downloaded structure collection.

Keep original, rederived, full-reference and leave-out variants distinct during
reproduction. Changing smoothing, contact definitions or composition weighting
creates a different potential and requires its own provenance and matched comparison.
