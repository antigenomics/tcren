## Mathematical / biophysical interpretation requiring author ruling

- **Measured energy correspondence is a result; a direct ergodicity test is an additional interpretation.** `main.tex:602–616` and `supplementary.tex:339–352` equate the cross-complex regression with testing an ensemble/time-average assumption and licensing single-structure ranking. Recorded macros give R²=0.460 across 184 complexes (`results/data/erg_numbers.tex:4,38`) and the independent kinetics association R=-0.5398 across 23 complexes (`native_numbers.tex:70,75`). Preserve these results. A cross-complex regression is not by itself a demonstration of stationary ensemble sampling or ergodicity; the kinetics association likewise does not establish a kinetic transition-state model. Escalated to parent; no prose was rewritten and no control proposed for execution.
- **A conditional mean on the transformed confidence axis is not automatically the mean on its original axis.** Benchmark `confidence_rescale.py:95–96` calculates sigmoid(mu + sd × E[z|x]), labels it `E[conf | descriptors]` at line 86, and returns original confidence units. In general E[sigmoid(Z)|x] differs from sigmoid(E[Z|x]); this expression is a plug-in transform (and under a symmetric Gaussian, transformed conditional median). Parent/library reviewer should reconcile the API definition and manuscript notation, preserving measured ranking results.
- **Do not resurrect the already-corrected derivation-set objection from old instructions.** Benchmark CLAUDE's open loop still describes inverse-epitope-only weighting and an old crystal count, whereas current manuscript `supplementary.tex:151–166` explicitly gives the mean of inverse epitope and receptor frequencies and filters withdrawn entries. Current generated counts and shipped metadata need the owner check; the historical paragraph alone is not evidence of a current paper error.
